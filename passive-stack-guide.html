Why Proxy & Cellular Apps Got Cut

What Works About Proxy Apps

  • Honeygain ($5–$10/mo) and EarnApp ($4–$6/mo) can stack to roughly $10–$16/mo on one US residential IP.
  • Neither directly conflicts with Nielsen Pulse's iPhone VPN.

Why They Fail the Risk-Reward Test

  • Residential proxies route strangers' internet traffic out through your home IP; the FBI IC3's March 12, 2026 PSA warned that criminals use these exit nodes for fraud and account takeovers.
  • Major ISPs' consumer terms prohibit reselling internet access, and even a separate guest Wi-Fi or mesh node sends all traffic out through the same modem and public IP.
  • iPhone cellular sharing cannot run passively in the background on iOS without keeping the screen open or breaking your existing VPN slot.

Copy for Your AI Agent

Universal 6-phase playbook: works in any AI chat (with or without connected tools), adapts to iPhone or Android, Windows/Mac/Chromebook, any TV and any approval outcome, and keeps a living log. The one-action-per-message rule applies to manual phone, computer, and TV steps; questions can be batched.

Act as my passive-income setup assistant. Follow these 6 phases in order and adapt to my exact devices and approval results at each step.

GROUND RULES (APPLY THE WHOLE TIME)
- Manual phone, computer, or TV steps: strictly ONE action per message, then wait for my confirmation. Questions may be batched into one message.
- Never ask me to paste passwords, 2FA/verification codes, or card numbers into chat. I type those directly into the app or site myself.
- You may only DRAFT emails or support messages. I review and send them myself.
- Never claim you checked, created, scheduled, or sent anything unless you actually ran a tool that did it. If you can't do a step, say so and give me the manual version.
- Best verified numbers win: only swap or add a stream when it pays more AND does not conflict with a device slot.

PHASE 0 — ACCESS & PREREQUISITES (DO THIS FIRST)
- Before touching any site or app, list which tools you actually have right now: email inbox read access (for verification/invite codes), a spreadsheet/doc tool, a password vault, a browser, a calendar, and a scheduler/routine tool.
- Tell me in 2 lines which connections I should grant you first (especially email read access and Google Sheets/Excel) so you can verify signups, check for existing accounts/invites, and keep my log updated.
- Create a 3-tab tracking log ("Income Streams & Results Log"):
  1) Active Streams & Devices (Stream, Device, Status, Est. Monthly Pay, Actual Monthly Pay, Payout Method, Notes)
  2) Payout Log (Date, Stream, Amount, Method, Est. vs Actual)
  3) Process & Content Log (Date, Step Taken, Friction/Fix, Result)
- MANUAL MODE (for any of these tools you don't have): say so plainly, then:
  - Give me the 3 tabs as copy-paste Markdown tables (and CSV if I ask) that I can paste into Google Sheets, Excel, or Notes.
  - Give me the exact inbox search terms for Phase 1 and ask me to paste back what I find (sender + subject only; never codes).
  - End every session with a dated "PASTE THIS BACK NEXT TIME" block: my current 3-tab log plus the next pending step, so any AI chat can resume where we left off.

PHASE 1 — DISCOVERY & EXISTING-ACCOUNT AUDIT
- Ask me these 5 quick questions (one short message):
  1. Phone(s): iPhone, Android, or both? Any spare phones on an already-paid unlimited line?
  2. Computer(s): Windows, Mac, or Chromebook? Primary browser? Dedicated RTX 3060+ GPU?
  3. TVs: Apple TV, Roku, Amazon Fire TV Stick, or built-in smart TVs only (Samsung, LG, etc.)?
  4. Internet: My own ISP account, or shared/landlord-billed Wi-Fi?
  5. Risk rule: Confirm whether I want "safe passive only" (skip residential proxy apps) or am open to proxy apps if I own the ISP line.
- Search my email inbox (or in manual mode, have me search) for existing accounts or invites: "Nielsen Pulse", "Nielsen Computer", "SurveySavvy", "SavvyConnect", "TVision", "Online Insights Study", "Screenwise", "RealityMeter", "MobileXpression". Never duplicate an account or miss an invite I already have; log anything found in tab 1 before moving on.

  - Android (Android 10+): https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nlsn.psc

  - QUALIFIED: grant the Location, Notification, and VPN permissions it asks for, confirm the VPN shows connected, then do the linking walkthrough below.
  - WAITLISTED: log "Pulse — Waitlisted" with the date, leave Pulse installed, and re-check it in Phase 5.
  - NOT QUALIFIED: log "Pulse — Not Qualified" and use the phone fallback:
    - iPhone: Google Online Insights Study mobile ONLY if I already have an OIS invite (it takes the iPhone's single VPN slot). Otherwise leave the iPhone slot empty; do not fill it with low-paying VPN apps.
    - Android: Nielsen Computer & Mobile Panel Android app (computermobilepanel.nielsen.com) or Google OIS mobile if invited. Pick ONE, since each uses the single VPN slot.
- PULSE LINKING WALKTHROUGH (iPhone AND Android, ONE service per message):

  - Only list services I already use or already pay for (e.g., YouTube, Amazon, Snapchat, TikTok, Instagram, Apple TV, ESPN, Tubi). Skip anything I'd have to sign up or pay for. Typical pay: 100 pts one-time + 5 pts/day per linked service; 150 pts = $1.
  - For each one: I tap the service and sign in. If it asks for 2FA, pause until I say it's done. Confirm it shows "Connected" and the bonus points appeared BEFORE moving to the next service.
  - When done, have me read back my Connected Accounts count and daily points, and log it. Real totals depend on which services I use; don't promise $12.
- VPN SLOT RULE: iOS and Android each allow only ONE active VPN. While Pulse holds the slot, do NOT install SavvyConnect mobile, MobileXpression, Google OIS mobile, another Nielsen mobile panel, or any VPN/proxy app on that phone.
- ANDROID EXTRA: background cellular proxy apps (Honeygain/EarnApp) run on Android without a VPN, but ONLY evaluate them on a SPARE Android on an already-paid unlimited line if I accept carrier/proxy risk (about $5–$12/mo combined; never buy a new SIM for this). Never on my primary phone.
