What the Hell Does That Mean? AI Jargon Translated

The "What Did He Just Say?" AI Vocabulary List. 25 AI terms you keep hearing, explained in plain English for small business owners.

1. LLM
That's just the fanciest autocomplete you've ever seen, finishing your sentences like it already knows what you're thinking.
2. Token
Those little word pieces the machine actually counts... and yes, they charge you for every single one.
3. Context Window
How much it can remember before it completely forgets who you are and starts over.
4. Hallucination
It just made the whole thing up and delivered it with full confidence, like it knows exactly what it's talking about.
5. RAG
Look it up first, then answer. Stop guessing and go check the source before you open your mouth.
6. Agent / Agentic
This AI doesn't just talk... it actually gets up and does the work for you.
7. Fine-tuning
Extra training so it stops being a generic know-it-all and gets good at your specific thing.
8. Parameters / Weights
All those millions or billions of tiny knobs they twisted while it was learning.
9. Training / Pre-training
The expensive phase where it read the entire internet and learned every pattern.
10. Inference
The actual answering part. Way cheaper once all that expensive learning is already done.
11. Prompt / Prompt Engineering
What you type. The "engineering" part is just getting good at asking so it stops giving you garbage.
12. Embedding / Vector
Turning words into numbers so the computer can measure exactly how similar two things are.
13. Transformer
The architecture that made modern AI stop sucking. Attention is the whole game.
14. Neural Network
A bunch of math layers loosely inspired by brains. Not actually a brain, though.
15. Generative AI
It makes new stuff (text, images, whatever) instead of just labeling the old stuff.
16. Alignment
Trying to make the AI do what humans actually want instead of what we accidentally told it.
17. Bias
Garbage in, garbage out. If the training data was skewed, the output is going to be skewed.
18. Zero-shot / Few-shot
It does the task with zero or just a few examples instead of needing a whole new training run.
19. Temperature
The creativity dial. Low is boring and safe. High is wild and unhinged.
20. Multimodal
Can handle text, images, and audio at the same time without missing a beat.
21. Diffusion
How most image generators work: start with pure noise and slowly clean it into a picture.
22. Open Weights
You can download the actual model and run it yourself instead of just renting it from the big companies.
23. Frontier Model
The current biggest, newest, most expensive ones from the top labs.
24. Guardrails
The rules and filters so it doesn't start saying insane or illegal stuff.
25. AI Slop
That low-effort AI garbage flooding the internet like nobody asked for it.
