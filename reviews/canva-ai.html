Canva AI (Magic Studio) review for small business

Canva AI is the free design tool that helps you make posts, flyers, and simple graphics without being a designer. Need a decent Facebook post or flyer without paying a designer every time? This gets you most of the way. Never make visuals? Skip it.

Verdict: Worth a try. Open this one when you need a post or flyer that doesn't look like it was made in Word. Use free for occasional stuff; pay only if you make graphics every week and keep hitting the AI limit.

What it's good for

  • Make a Facebook or Instagram post from a template.
  • Create a simple flyer or service card.
  • Write short captions or rewrite text inside a design with Magic Write.
  • Remove a background from a photo (check your plan).
  • Resize one design for different posts (check your plan).
  • Generate a basic image to drop into a graphic.

How to use it

1. Go to canva.com and sign up free.
2. Search for a template (example: "cleaning service Facebook post").
3. Click Magic Write or the AI tools and paste: "Rewrite this service description in plain English for a local Facebook post. Keep it short: [paste your text]."

What you'll get

A finished-looking post or flyer in a few minutes that you can edit and download. Free covers basic designs and a small AI allowance. More AI and premium stock cost money.

What you're missing

  • Free plan: up to 20 AI uses a month, shared across the AI tools. Bigger jobs burn more. No Ultra AI.
  • Pro is US$144/year for one person (up to 200 Premium AI uses a month). Business is US$250/year per person. See canva.com/pricing for monthly prices.
  • Some tools, like Background Remover and Magic Resize, may need Pro. Check canva.com/pricing before you count on them.
  • A free trial only gets the free AI allowance until it turns paid.
  • Not for complex logos, branding, or print-ready professional work. Check text, spacing, and whether it looks like your business.

Price

Free: Free: $0. Editor, templates, 1 Brand Kit, up to 20 AI uses a month.

Paid: Pro US$144/year for one person (up to 200 Premium or 20 Ultra AI uses a month); Business US$250/year per person; Enterprise through sales. Prices exclude tax. See canva.com/pricing for current pricing.
