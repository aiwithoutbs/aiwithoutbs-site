AI tool reviews

Honest AI tool reviews for small business

What each tool actually does for your business, what it costs, and what you are missing. If it won't move your business forward, we say skip it.

The six ranks, best to worst

Every review gets exactly one rank. No participation trophies. The bottom rank is on the board even though nothing has earned it yet.

1Incredible

Best in its class. Worth paying for if you need it.

2Must use

Get it today. It earns its keep from day one.

3Badass

Does one job really well. Point it at that job.

4It's alright

Useful in the right setup. Don't rearrange your life for it.

5Could be left alone

Only if you already live in it. Otherwise, skip.

6You're doing too much

More hype than help. Walk away.

The verdicts, ranked straight

ClaudeIncredible

Actually reads the whole damn contract.

Best when the wording has to hold up. When a proposal, an agreement, or a hard email has to say exactly what you mean, this is the one we point at it.

ChatGPTMust use

Kills the blank page in 30 seconds.

Free, and it actually gets you a draft. Still check the facts.

PerplexityBadass

Answers with receipts, links included.

Answers you can check before you bet on them.

Canva AIBadass

Flyers that look like you paid somebody.

Wins at posts and flyers. Free AI uses run out fast.

GrokBadass

Knows what happened this morning.

Free, live info, usable draft. Don't treat it as an expert.

Zapier AIBadass

Quit copy-pasting like it's 1997.

Kills repetitive work. Start with one task.

MidjourneyBadass

Pictures that look like you hired a designer.

Top-shelf images, no free plan. Public unless you pay for Pro.

DeepLBadass

Translations that read like a person wrote them.

Does translation really well. Free to start; paid keeps your text private.

CalendlyBadass

Ends the "does Tuesday work?" email chain.

Free booking link that works. The AI notetaker costs extra.

LoomBadass

Show it once on video instead of typing it out.

Free screen videos that work. The AI costs $24 a user a month.

Otter.aiIt's alright

Takes the call notes so you don't.

Great for notes. The free plan dies after about 30 minutes.

Google GeminiIt's alright

Your AI if you live in Gmail.

Useful if you're already in Google. Free is limited.

Microsoft CopilotIt's alright

Your AI if you live in Word and Outlook.

Only really useful inside the apps, and that takes paid.

Muse (Meta)It's alright

The AI that gets off the couch.

Promising, limited availability. Make it prove it.

Make.comIt's alright

Cheaper automation, if you put in the time.

More automation per dollar than Zapier. Expect a learning curve.

Adobe FireflyIt's alright

AI pictures you can use without a lawyer.

Free to try, safe for ads. Video eats credits fast.

QuickBooks AIIt's alright

Your bookkeeper gets a sidekick, not a replacement.

Handy if you already use QuickBooks. The best AI costs $140 a month.

TidioIt's alright

Answers the same customer questions so you don't have to.

Handy if customers ask the same things. Watch the monthly chat cap.

Intercom FinIt's alright

Pays for itself only when it solves the problem.

Strong AI support agent at $0.99 per solved chat. Seats and add-ons add up.

CapCutIt's alright

Captions your videos so you don't have to type them.

Free captions and quick edits. Check the license before using templates in ads.

Zoom AI CompanionIt's alright

Writes the meeting recap so you don't have to.

Free with Zoom Pro: unlimited meeting summaries. The $20 ZoomMate add-on is optional.

Mailchimp AIIt's alright

Gets your newsletter past the blank page.

Free extra on Standard and Premium. Not a reason to upgrade.

SynthesiaIt's alright

Training videos without getting on camera.

Free to try with a watermark. Starter is $19 a month and runs on credits.

HeyGenIt's alright

Videos of you, without filming you every time.

Free to try with a watermark. Creator is $29 a month and runs on credits.

Notion AICould be left alone

Finds the stuff you swore you saved.

Only if you already live in Notion. Don't start a $100/month system for the AI.
