Midjourney review for small business

Midjourney turns a sentence into a picture that looks like somebody paid a designer. It does one job, images (and now short videos), and it does it well. There's no free plan, and your images are public unless you pay for Pro.

#3Our rank · see the six-rank keyBadass

“Pictures that look like you hired a designer.” Does one job really well. Point it at that job. Researched

WHY YOU SHOULD CARE

If you post on social media, run ads, or need pictures for a website or flyer, Midjourney can make custom images instead of stock photos everybody else is using.

You type what you want, and it gives you options in a minute or so.

If you rarely need images, or you already pay for Canva, skip it.

WHEN TO USE IT

  • You need fresh images for posts, ads, or your website every week.
  • You're tired of the same stock photos your competitors use.
  • You want to see a few visual ideas before paying a designer or photographer.
  • You care more about how good the picture looks than about speed of layout.

WHEN TO SKIP IT

  • You only need an image now and then. Free tools like Canva's AI or Microsoft Designer may be enough.
  • You need flyers and posts with lots of text and layout. Canva is better for that.
  • You need your images kept private but don't want to pay $60 a month for Pro.

HOW TO USE IT

Step 1: Go to midjourney.com, sign in, and pick a plan. There's no free trial on the website.

Step 2: Type a description in the prompt box. Be specific about the subject, setting, light, and style.

Step 3: Pick the best of the results, then use the buttons to upscale or vary it.

Ready-to-paste prompt:

Bright, friendly photo-style image of a small-town hardware store counter, a smiling older owner helping a customer, warm morning light through the front window, clean and uncluttered, room at the top for a headline --ar 16:9

WHAT YOU'LL GET

A set of image options in the style you described, usually good enough to post after a couple of tries.

Text inside images, exact logos, and specific real people are hit and miss. Plan on tweaking your prompt a few times.

We have not run our own test images for this review.

WHAT YOU'RE MISSING

Here's how the money works.

Midjourney bills a monthly or yearly subscription. Each plan comes with a set amount of fast GPU time. That's the computer time used to make your images.

PlanMonthlyYearlyWhat you get
Basic$10$96 ($8/mo)About 200 fast minutes a month
Standard$30$288 ($24/mo)15 fast hours, unlimited Relax images
Pro$60$576 ($48/mo)30 fast hours, Relax images and SD video, Stealth mode
Mega$120$1,152 ($96/mo)60 fast hours, Relax images and SD video, Stealth mode

The catches:

  • Plans renew automatically until you cancel.
  • Relax mode is slower but unlimited. Basic doesn't get it.
  • Extra fast time costs $4 an hour on any plan.
  • Companies grossing over $1 million a year need Pro or Mega, per Midjourney's plan page.

Bottom line: Start with Basic for a month. Move to Standard only if you keep running out.

PRIVACY

Midjourney is public by default. Midjourney's help center says every image and video you make can potentially be found on its Explore page.

If you're making images for an unannounced product, a client, or anything private, you need Stealth mode, and that's only on Pro and Mega.

Don't upload customer photos or confidential material on Basic or Standard. Read Midjourney's Terms of Service before using images for paying clients.

ALTERNATIVES

  • Canva AI — Easier for flyers and social posts with text and layout.
  • Microsoft Designer — Free option if you only need images now and then.
  • Adobe Firefly — Fits if you already pay for Adobe.

Price

Free: No free plan. No free trial on midjourney.com or Discord, per Midjourney's help center. A limited trial exists only in the niji·journey app for iOS and Android.

Paid: Basic $10/month ($96/year), Standard $30/month ($288/year), Pro $60/month ($576/year), Mega $120/month ($1,152/year). Annual plans are 20% off and paid up front. Extra fast time is $4 an hour.

VERDICT

Badass Rank 3 of 6: Does one job really well. Point it at that job.

Midjourney is one of the best pure image makers out there. Point it at that job.

If you need custom images every week, Basic or Standard pays for itself against stock photos. If you need a flyer with words on it, use Canva and skip this.

Watch the privacy: on Basic and Standard, assume anyone can see what you make.
