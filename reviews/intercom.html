Intercom Fin review for small business

Fin is Intercom's AI agent that answers customer questions by chat, email, and more, and you pay $0.99 each time it actually solves one. Strong tool, built for real support teams. For a small shop, the per-answer bill plus seats adds up.

#4Our rank · see the six-rank keyIt's alright

“Pays for itself only when it solves the problem.” Useful in the right setup. Don't rearrange your life for it. Researched

WHY YOU SHOULD CARE

If customer questions pile up faster than you can answer them, Fin can take the routine ones on chat, email, and other channels, then hand the rest to a person. You only pay when it gets the job done, and you can set a monthly spending limit.

If you get a handful of customer questions a week, skip it.

WHEN TO USE IT

  • Customer questions eat hours of your week.
  • You already use a helpdesk like Zendesk or Freshdesk and want AI on top.
  • You have solid help articles or an FAQ it can learn from.
  • You want to pay only when the AI actually solves something.

WHEN TO SKIP IT

  • You get only a few customer questions a week.
  • Your customers mostly call instead of typing.
  • You have no FAQ or help articles to feed it yet.
  • You want a flat, predictable monthly bill with no per-answer charges.

HOW TO USE IT

Step 1: Start the free 14-day trial at fin.ai or intercom.com/pricing. Add-ons are included in the trial, per Intercom.

Step 2: Feed Fin your help articles or FAQ, connect your website chat or support email, and set your monthly spending limit right away.

Step 3: Ask it your 10 most common customer questions yourself. Fix any wrong answer before real customers see it.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

An AI agent that answers routine questions and passes the hard ones to you or your team. Each solved conversation costs $0.99, charged once per conversation at most.

Intercom publishes big resolution numbers from big customers. Those aren't your numbers. We have not run our own tests for this review.

WHAT YOU'RE MISSING

Here's how the money works.

PiecePriceWhat you get
Fin AI Agent$0.99 per outcomeAI answers on chat, email, and more
Essential seat$19/seat/mo yearly (new customers; list $29)Inbox, ticketing, help center, Messenger
Advanced seat$85/seat/mo yearlyWorkflows, multiple inboxes, 20 free Lite seats
Expert seat$132/seat/mo yearlySSO, HIPAA support, SLAs, 50 free Lite seats
Fin Voice$1.99 per voice outcomeThrough sales

Add-ons: Copilot $29 per agent/mo, Pro from $100/mo, Proactive Support Plus $99/mo.

The catches:

  • You pay nothing if Fin can't answer or the customer asks for a human, per Intercom.
  • An outcome also counts if the customer simply doesn't ask for more help.
  • Using Fin on another helpdesk has a monthly minimum.

Bottom line: Set a spending limit on day one, run the trial on real questions, then do the math against your actual monthly chat count.

PRIVACY

Intercom says you can set spending limits and alerts, and Fin hands off to a person when asked.

We did not review Intercom's data and AI training policy for this review [CHECK]. Read it before you let Fin handle customer account or payment questions.

ALTERNATIVES

  • Tidio — Cheaper website chat with its Lyro AI agent and a free plan. Better fit for a small shop. Read our Tidio review.
  • A good FAQ page — Free, and it may cut your questions on its own.
  • ChatGPT or Claude — Can help you write that FAQ, but won't answer customers on your site.

Price

Free: No free plan. Free 14-day trial, per Intercom's Fin pricing page. Startups with under $10M raised and 15 or fewer employees can apply for a steep first-year discount.

Paid: Fin AI Agent: $0.99 per outcome, at most once per conversation. Intercom helpdesk seats (billed yearly): Essential $19/seat/mo for new customers (list $29), Advanced $85, Expert $132. Month-to-month prices weren't captured [CHECK]. Checked Nov 20, 2026.

VERDICT

It's alright Rank 4 of 6: Useful in the right setup. Don't rearrange your life for it.

A serious support agent with fair pay-for-results pricing. Worth it once your support volume is real. A small shop with a few chats a day can start cheaper with Tidio.
