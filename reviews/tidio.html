Tidio review for small business

Tidio puts a chat box on your website, and its Lyro AI agent answers customer questions from your own content, then hands the hard ones to you. Worth a look if your phone and inbox fill up with the same five questions. Watch the conversation limits.

#4Our rank · see the six-rank keyIt's alright

“Answers the same customer questions so you don't have to.” Useful in the right setup. Don't rearrange your life for it. Researched

WHY YOU SHOULD CARE

If customers keep asking the same questions (hours, shipping, pricing, returns), Lyro can answer them on your site and by email while you're busy or asleep. It learns from your website, FAQ, and product catalog, and hands off to a human when it should.

If you don't get many customer questions online, skip it.

WHEN TO USE IT

  • Customers ask you the same questions over and over.
  • You get website or email questions after hours.
  • You run an online store (Tidio lists native Shopify actions on Growth).
  • You have a decent FAQ or can write one.

WHEN TO SKIP IT

  • Your website gets few visitors or almost no chats.
  • Your customers call, they don't type.
  • Every question needs a custom quote or a judgment call.

HOW TO USE IT

Step 1: Sign up at tidio.com. Tidio says no credit card is needed, and you get a 7-day trial plus 50 one-time free Lyro conversations.

Step 2: Point Lyro at your website and FAQ so it learns from your own content, then add the chat widget to your site.

Step 3: Open the chat yourself and ask it your 5 most common customer questions. Fix any answer it gets wrong before real customers see it.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

A chat box that can answer your routine questions around the clock and pass the rest to you or your team.

Tidio says Lyro only answers from the content you give it, so it's only as good as your FAQ. We have not run our own tests for this review.

The catches:

  • AI and human chats count against the same monthly cap.
  • A conversation counts once your team or Lyro replies, no matter how many messages.
  • Add-ons stack up fast.

Bottom line: Start on the free plan and the trial, count how many chats you actually get, then pick the smallest tier that fits.

PRIVACY

Tidio says Lyro works only from the content and guidance you give it, and you can watch conversations live and jump in anytime.

We did not review Tidio's data and AI training policy for this review [CHECK]. Read it before you let it handle customer account or payment questions.

ALTERNATIVES

  • Intercom Fin — Another AI support agent, aimed at bigger support teams [CHECK pricing].
  • A good FAQ page — Free, and it may cut your questions on its own.
  • ChatGPT or Claude — Can help you write that FAQ, but won't sit on your website and answer customers.

Price
