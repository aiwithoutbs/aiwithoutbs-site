Zapier AI review for small business

Zapier AI is the free tool that connects your apps and helps you build simple automations without writing code. Want a new customer form to land in a spreadsheet and email you a note? This wires it up. Never need apps talking to each other? Skip it.

Verdict: Worth a try. Open this one when you want two apps to talk so you stop copying and pasting. Use free for one simple automation; pay only if you hit the task limit or need more than two steps.

What it's good for

  • Send a new lead into a spreadsheet and a text to your phone.
  • Have form answers emailed to you automatically.
  • Move info from one app to another without copy-paste.
  • Describe what you want in plain English and let Zapier's AI draft the Zap.
  • Add an AI step that drafts a reply or summary inside a workflow (paid plans).
  • Test a small automation free before you pay.

How to use it

1. Go to zapier.com and sign up free.
2. Click Create and describe what you want: "When a new Google Form comes in, add the answers to a Google Sheet and email me."
3. Let the AI suggest the steps, then test it with one real form.

What you'll get

A working automation that moves data between apps. Free handles basic two-step Zaps. Multi-step workflows and AI steps cost money and eat your monthly task allowance.

What you're missing

  • Free is 100 tasks a month and two-step Zaps only, with no AI steps, filters, or paths.
  • Professional starts at $19.99/month billed yearly ($29.99 monthly) for 750 tasks. Team starts at $69/month yearly. See zapier.com/pricing.
  • AI steps can cost more than one task each, so costs climb fast if you lean on them.
  • Still not a developer. Complex logic or custom apps need more setup or help.
  • Not for chat or research. ChatGPT or Claude are better there.

Price

Free: Free: $0. 100 tasks a month, two-step Zaps only, AI Zap builder with a daily message limit. 14-day Pro trial, no card.

Paid: Professional from $19.99/month billed yearly ($29.99 monthly) for 750 tasks; Team from $69/month yearly ($103.50 monthly) for 2,000 tasks. Overage on monthly plans costs extra.
