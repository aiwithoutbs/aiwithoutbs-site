Calendly review for small business

Calendly kills the "does Tuesday work?" email chain. Send a link, they pick a time, it lands on your calendar. The free plan covers one meeting type. The new AI pieces (Notetaker and Callie) cost extra.

#3Our rank · see the six-rank keyBadass

“Ends the "does Tuesday work?" email chain.” Does one job really well. Point it at that job. Researched

WHY YOU SHOULD CARE

Every "how about Thursday at 2?" email you send is time you don't bill. Calendly checks your calendar, shows only the times you're free, and books the meeting for you, reminders included.

If you take two meetings a month, skip it.

WHEN TO USE IT

  • You book calls, consults, or estimates every week.
  • Leads find you online and you want them to book without waiting on you.
  • You want deposits or payments taken at booking.
  • No-shows cost you money and you want automatic reminders.

WHEN TO SKIP IT

  • You only set a few meetings a month.
  • Your customers would rather call than click a link.
  • Your calendar already has a free booking page you're happy with [CHECK: Google Calendar and Microsoft Bookings offer one on some plans].
  • You only want AI meeting notes. A dedicated notetaker may fit better.

HOW TO USE IT

Step 1: Sign up free at calendly.com and connect your Google or Outlook calendar.

Step 2: Set your hours and add a 15-minute buffer between meetings (a buffer is a cushion of free time so calls don't run back to back).

Step 3: Copy your booking link into your email signature. Next time someone asks "when are you free?", send the link instead.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

A booking page where people pick an open time and the meeting shows up on your calendar, no back-and-forth. Paying unlocks reminders, more meeting types, and payments.

Calendly publishes customer quotes about time saved. Those aren't your numbers. We have not run our own tests for this review.

WHAT YOU'RE MISSING

Here's how the money works.

The catches:

  • Only hosts need a seat. The people booking with you don't pay or need an account.
  • The AI add-ons are English only, and Callie is in beta.
  • Yearly billing saves up to 20%, per Calendly. You pay a year up front.

Bottom line: Start free. Upgrade to Standard the day you want reminders or payments. Only pay for Plus if you don't already have something taking meeting notes.

PRIVACY

Calendly lists data deletion tools, and SSO (single sign-on, one company login for everything) on Teams as an add-on and Enterprise.

We did not review Calendly's data and AI training policy for this review [CHECK]. Read it before you let Notetaker record calls with clients.

ALTERNATIVES

  • Otter.ai — If all you want is AI meeting notes, not scheduling. Read our Otter.ai review.
  • Google Calendar booking pages or Microsoft Bookings — May already come with your email plan [CHECK].
  • Zapier — Can push new Calendly bookings into your other apps. Read our Zapier AI review.

Price

Free: forever plan: one meeting type, one calendar connection, one-on-one scheduling, a booking page, and the browser extension. Paid plans come with a 14-day trial, then drop you back to Free automatically, per Calendly.

Paid: Per seat per month (only the host needs a seat; the people booking don't): Standard $10 yearly / $12 monthly. Standard Plus (adds Notetaker and Callie) $18 / $24. Teams $16 / $20. Teams Plus $24 / $32. Enterprise starts at $15,000 a year for 50 seats. Checked Nov 21, 2026.
