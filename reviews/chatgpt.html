ChatGPT review for small business

ChatGPT is the free AI that knocks out the first draft so you don't stare at a blank screen. It spits out a decent email, post, or summary in 30 seconds so you can get on with the real work. If you don't mind the blank page, skip it.

Verdict: Worth a try. Keeps you off the blank page when a customer email or post is waiting. Use it for the first draft, then fix it yourself.

What it's good for

  • Draft a reply to an angry customer email so you don't fire one off hot.
  • Turn rough notes into a clean client update or estimate description.
  • Write a short Facebook or Nextdoor post about a service you offer.
  • Summarize a long email or document so you know the main points.
  • Make a simple image for a flyer or post.
  • Answer everyday questions about prices, rules, or how something works.

How to use it

1. Go to chatgpt.com and start a free chat.
2. Paste this prompt: "Write a short, calm reply to this customer email. Keep it under 100 words and end with a clear next step: [paste the email]."
3. Read it, change anything that doesn't sound like you, then send.

What you'll get

A usable first draft or quick answer in under a minute. Check names, prices, and facts before you use it. Free handles basic writing; more use, better models, and more images cost money.

What you're missing

  • Free plan limits messages, uploads, image creation, voice, and memory. OpenAI doesn't publish exact numbers, just "limited."
  • It can sound confident when it's wrong on numbers, dates, or fast-moving topics.
  • Paid plans: Go $8/month, Plus $20/month, Pro $200/month. Monthly billing only. See chatgpt.com for current pricing.
  • Not the best pick for long, careful document work. Claude is often better there.
  • Your chats can be used to train their models unless you turn it off in Data Controls.
  • Double-check anything about money, legal, or medical.

Price

Free: Free: $0, for everyone. Limited messages, uploads, images, and memory (no exact limits published).

Paid: Go $8/month (US); Plus $20/month; Pro $200/month. Business is priced per user (2+ users); Enterprise through sales. Monthly billing only for Go, Plus, Pro. See chatgpt.com for current pricing.
