ChatGPT review for small business

The AI that knocks out a decent first draft in about 30 seconds so you stop staring at a blank screen like it owes you money. AI's here to stay. Quit whining and let it take the first swing.

Researched Verdict: Worth a try.

WHY YOU SHOULD CARE

You've got better things to do than wrestle a blank page. This one spits out a usable email, post, or summary fast enough that you can fix it and move on. Free to start. More use and better results cost money. It still talks like it knows more than it does. You still make the final call.

WHEN TO USE IT

  • Turn rough notes into a clean client update or estimate description.
  • Write a short Facebook or Nextdoor post about a service you offer.
  • Summarize a long email or document so you know the main points.
  • Make a simple image for a flyer or post.
  • Answer everyday questions about prices, rules, or how something works.
  • Get something on the page when you're stuck and the clock is running.

WHEN TO SKIP IT

  • Long, careful document work where the wording has to be exact. Claude usually handles that cleaner.
  • Anything where being confidently wrong on numbers, dates, or fast-moving topics will cost you.
  • Heavy daily use if you refuse to pay.

HOW TO USE IT

1 Go to chatgpt.com and start a free chat.

2 Paste this prompt: "Turn these rough notes into a clean client update. Keep it short, plain, and focused on what got done and what's next: [paste notes]."

3 Read it, change anything that doesn't sound like you, then send.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

A usable first draft or quick answer in under a minute. Check names, prices, and facts before you use it. Free handles basic writing; more use, better models, and more images cost money.

WHAT YOU'RE MISSING

Free plan limits messages, uploads, image creation, voice, and memory. OpenAI doesn't publish exact numbers, just "limited." It can sound confident when it's wrong on numbers, dates, or fast-moving topics. Paid plans: Go $8/month (US); Plus $20/month; Pro $200/month. Business is priced per user (2+ users); Enterprise through sales. Monthly billing only for Go, Plus, Pro. See chatgpt.com for current pricing. Not the best pick for long, careful document work. Your chats can be used to train their models unless you turn it off in Data Controls. Double-check anything about money, legal, or medical.

PRIVACY

Chats can be used to train their models unless you turn it off in Data Controls; don't dump sensitive client or financial details you wouldn't want floating around.

ALTERNATIVES

Claude: better when the document is long and the wording has to sound like a grown adult wrote it. Grok: handier if you also want live stuff from X and the web in the same chat.

Price

Free: Free: $0, for everyone. Limited messages, uploads, images, and memory (no exact limits published).

Paid: Go $8/month (US); Plus $20/month; Pro $200/month. Business is priced per user (2+ users); Enterprise through sales. Monthly billing only for Go, Plus, Pro. See chatgpt.com for current pricing.

VERDICT

Worth a try.

It's free and it actually gets you off the blank page when a customer email or post is waiting. Just remember it will still occasionally hand you a perfectly confident answer that's missing the one detail that actually matters.
