Perplexity review for small business

The AI that answers your question and then shows you the links so you can see if it's full of it. Here's the deal: use this when you need an answer you can actually check instead of trusting some chatbot's smooth story.

Researched Verdict: Worth a try.

WHY YOU SHOULD CARE

Most of these tools will hand you a confident answer and hope you don't look too hard. This one at least has the decency to show you the sources. Good when you need a competitor's pricing, a local rule, or current info and you want to click the damn links yourself. Free covers everyday questions. Deep Research costs money. Sources can still be wrong. You still make the final call.

WHEN TO USE IT

  • Get a short answer with sources you can click and check.
  • Research a competitor, a service area, or a new rule in plain English.
  • Compare two options and see where the info came from.
  • Summarize current news or local conditions with links.
  • Double-check a claim before you put it in a proposal or post.
  • Find quick facts without digging through ten search results.

WHEN TO SKIP IT

  • Long careful writing or creative drafts. Claude or ChatGPT are stronger there.
  • You just need a quick email or post and don't care about sources.
  • Heavy daily use if you refuse to pay.

HOW TO USE IT

1 Go to perplexity.ai.

2 Paste this prompt: "What are the current average prices for house cleaning in Charlotte, NC? Give me a short answer and list the sources."

3 Click the links, read what matters, and use only what you can verify.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

A short answer with numbered sources in under a minute. Click through and check them. Free handles everyday questions; Deep Research and model choice cost money.

WHAT YOU'RE MISSING

Free gets 3 Pro Searches a day. Basic searches are "practically unlimited." No model choice, connectors, or image generation on free. Pro is $17/month billed yearly. The monthly price shows at checkout. Max is $167/month billed yearly. See perplexity.ai for current pricing. Not the best for long careful writing or creative drafts. Still not a lawyer or accountant. Double-check anything that costs you money or binds you. Sources can be wrong or outdated. Always click and verify.

PRIVACY

Chats can be used to improve the models unless you adjust settings; don't dump sensitive client or financial details you wouldn't want floating around.

ALTERNATIVES

Claude: better when you need the answer rewritten into careful, human-sounding business language. Grok: better if you also want what people are saying on X right now mixed in.

Price

Free: Free: $0. Cited answers, practically unlimited basic searches, 3 Pro Searches a day, limited file uploads.

Paid: Pro $17/month billed yearly (monthly price shown at checkout): Deep Research, model choice, image/video generation. Max $167/month billed yearly. Enterprise plans for teams.

Worth a try.
