Perplexity review for small business

Perplexity is the free AI that answers questions and shows you the sources so you can check them yourself. Need a real answer with links, like a competitor's pricing, a rule, or local info? This one is cleaner than a regular chatbot. Only need emails or posts written? Skip it.

Researched Verdict: Worth a try. Open this one when you need an answer you can check, not a smooth-sounding draft. Use free for quick research; pay only if you keep hitting the Pro Search limit.

What it's good for

  • Get a short answer with sources you can click and check.
  • Research a competitor, a service area, or a new rule in plain English.
  • Compare two options and see where the info came from.
  • Summarize current news or local conditions with links.
  • Double-check a claim before you put it in a proposal or post.
  • Find quick facts without digging through ten search results.

How to use it

1. Go to perplexity.ai.
2. Paste this prompt: "What are the current average prices for house cleaning in Charlotte, NC? Give me a short answer and list the sources."
3. Click the links, read what matters, and use only what you can verify.

What you'll get

A short answer with numbered sources in under a minute. Click through and check them. Free handles everyday questions; Deep Research and model choice cost money.

What you're missing

  • Free gets 3 Pro Searches a day. Basic searches are "practically unlimited." No model choice, connectors, or image generation on free.
  • Pro is $17/month billed yearly. The monthly price shows at checkout. Max is $167/month billed yearly. See perplexity.ai for current pricing.
  • Not the best for long careful writing or creative drafts. Claude or ChatGPT are stronger there.
  • Still not a lawyer or accountant. Double-check anything that costs you money or binds you.
  • Sources can be wrong or outdated. Always click and verify.

Price

Free: Free: $0. Cited answers, practically unlimited basic searches, 3 Pro Searches a day, limited file uploads.

Paid: Pro $17/month billed yearly (monthly price shown at checkout): Deep Research, model choice, image/video generation. Max $167/month billed yearly. Enterprise plans for teams.
