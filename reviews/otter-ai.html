Otter.ai review for small business

The free tool that turns your Zoom or phone calls into written notes so you don't have to type them. Here's the deal: use it when you hate writing notes after customer calls, but don't pretend the free version is going to handle long meetings.

Researched Verdict: Worth a try.

WHY YOU SHOULD CARE

You've got better things to do than type up what was said on a call. This hands you a transcript and a short summary. Free covers short customer calls. Longer meetings and more minutes cost money. Accuracy still drops when people talk over each other. You still make the final call.

WHEN TO USE IT

  • Get a written record of a customer call without typing.
  • Catch what was said on a Zoom, Teams, or Google Meet call.
  • Pull out action items so nothing gets forgotten.
  • Search old conversations for what a client said.
  • Record an in-person job walkthrough and turn it into notes.
  • Share a clean summary instead of a long email.

WHEN TO SKIP IT

  • Rarely on calls and don't need transcripts.
  • Heavy daily use if you refuse to pay.
  • Situations with heavy accents, background noise, or people talking over each other.

HOW TO USE IT

1 Go to otter.ai and sign up free.

2 Connect it to Zoom, Teams, or Google Meet (or open the app and hit record on your phone).

3 After the call, open the transcript and use the AI chat: "Give me a short summary and list the action items from this call."

WHAT YOU'LL GET

A written transcript and a short summary with action items in a few minutes. Works best on clear audio with one or two people. Free handles short calls; longer meetings and more minutes cost money.

WHAT YOU'RE MISSING

Free (Basic) is 300 minutes a month, cuts any single meeting off at 30 minutes, and gives 20 AI chat questions a month. Pro is $16.99/month, or $8.49/month billed yearly (1,200 minutes, up to 90 minutes per meeting). Business is $30/month or $24/month yearly. Accuracy drops with heavy accents, background noise, or people talking over each other. Recording other people? Check your state's consent laws first. Still not a lawyer. Double-check anything that costs you money or binds you.

PRIVACY

Recordings and transcripts can be used to improve the models unless you adjust settings; don't dump sensitive client details you wouldn't want floating around. Check consent laws before recording other people.

ALTERNATIVES

Claude: better if you just need a clean summary of notes you already have. ChatGPT: stronger if you want to rewrite the summary into something more polished after.

Price

Free: Basic: $0. Joins Zoom, Teams, and Google Meet; 300 minutes a month; 30 minutes per meeting; 20 AI chat questions a month; 3 lifetime file imports.

Paid: Pro $16.99/month, or $8.49/month billed yearly (1,200 min/month, 90 min per meeting). Business $30/month or $24/month billed yearly. Enterprise through sales. Yearly discount is for first-time subscribers.

VERDICT

Worth a try.

Open this one when you need notes from a call and don't want to type them. Just don't expect free to keep up once your calls start running past 30 minutes.
