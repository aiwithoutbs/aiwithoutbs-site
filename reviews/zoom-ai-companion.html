Zoom AI Companion review for small business

Zoom's AI Companion, now branded ZoomMate on Zoom's own pages, writes meeting summaries and answers questions about your Zoom calls. The free plan gives you 3 summarized meetings a month. Zoom Pro ($16.99 a user a month, or $14.16 billed yearly) makes summaries unlimited. The full ZoomMate add-on, with agents and workflows, is another $20 a user a month and runs on credits.

#4Our rank · see the six-rank keyIt's alright

“Writes the meeting recap so you don't have to.” Useful in the right setup. Don't rearrange your life for it. Researched

WHY YOU SHOULD CARE

If your sales calls, client check-ins, and team meetings already happen on Zoom, this writes the recap so you don't have to. On a paid Pro plan, meeting summaries are unlimited at no extra charge.

If you don't run your meetings on Zoom, skip it.

WHEN TO USE IT

  • Your meetings already run on Zoom.
  • You already pay for Zoom Pro or Business, so summaries cost nothing extra.
  • You forget to write down who promised what on calls.
  • You want one tool for meetings, chat, and notes instead of adding another app.

WHEN TO SKIP IT

  • Your meetings run on Google Meet or Teams.
  • You're on free Zoom and have more than 3 meetings a month worth summarizing. Pay for Pro or use a dedicated notetaker.
  • You don't want an AI listening to client calls, or your clients don't.
  • You're tempted by the ZoomMate add-on but don't have a real workflow for agents to run.

HOW TO USE IT

Step 1: Sign in to Zoom and host a meeting. Free Basic accounts get summaries for 3 hosted meetings a month, per Zoom's pricing page.

Step 2: Turn on the meeting summary from the AI Companion / ZoomMate control in the meeting [CHECK: the button label may have changed with the ZoomMate rename]. Tell everyone on the call that the AI is taking notes.

Step 3: Near the end, ask it this, then compare the answer to what you heard:

What did we agree to, who owns each next step, and by when?

WHAT YOU'LL GET

A written summary of the meeting and answers to questions about what was said, inside Zoom. Treat it as a first draft: read it before you send it to a client.

We have not run our own tests for this review.

WHAT YOU'RE MISSING

Here's how the money works, as listed on Zoom's pricing pages.

The catches:

  • The ZoomMate add-on runs on credits. Zoom says some features don't use credits, but AI actions and agents do. Watch the meter.
  • Zoom's ZoomMate pricing page lists 40-minute, 100-person meetings under the ZoomMate plan, which suggests the add-on alone doesn't include Pro meetings [CHECK].
  • "Billed yearly" prices mean paying for the year up front.

Bottom line: If you're on Zoom Pro, use the summaries you already pay for. Hold off on the add-on until you can name the job you'd give it.

PRIVACY

ZoomMate connects to your meetings, chats, calendar, and apps like Google Drive, Salesforce, and Jira, per Zoom's product page, and it keeps a "memory" of your past interactions. Zoom says its answers are "permission-aware."

We did not read Zoom's AI privacy terms for this review [CHECK: whether Zoom uses your meeting content to train AI, and how long it keeps it]. Let people know when the AI is taking notes, and keep confidential client calls out of it until you've read Zoom's terms.

VERDICT

It's alright Rank 4 of 6: Useful in the right setup. Don't rearrange your life for it.

If you already pay for Zoom Pro, the unlimited meeting summaries are a free win, so turn them on. The $20-a-user ZoomMate add-on is credit-metered agent stuff most small businesses don't need yet, which keeps this at "It's alright."
