Muse (Meta) review for small business

Most AI tools talk a big game. Muse wants to get off the couch, open your apps, and actually do something. Now THAT is an employee who better have a good performance review.

Researched Verdict: Worth a try.

WHY YOU SHOULD CARE

Meet Muse, Meta's AI agent (software that can carry out tasks for you).

Instead of just answering questions, Muse can work inside connected apps and on the web, with your approval.

Need a Facebook Marketplace listing? Give it pictures and details. It can put together a title, price, and description.

Need a customer email? It can draft one for you to review.

Need a reminder? It can help with that, too.

The interesting part is that Muse is designed to take action, not just give you another paragraph explaining how you should take action.

But before you start handing over the keys to the company, remember: You still need to supervise it.

WHEN TO USE IT

  • Sell products: Give Muse pictures and details and have it draft a Facebook Marketplace listing.
  • Improve listings: Ask it to make product descriptions clearer and more appealing.
  • Create advertisements: Put the title, price, and description together before posting.
  • Customer communication: Have it draft customer emails for you to review.
  • Business reminders: Set reminders and maintain an ongoing to-do conversation.
  • Follow-up work: Let it pull details from connected apps to help with simple follow-ups.

WHEN TO SKIP IT

  • You never sell products, hand off tasks, or need an AI agent to take action.
  • You need guaranteed availability everywhere. Muse is still in limited testing and is not available everywhere.
  • You want a shared AI system for an entire company. Muse is designed for individual use, not shared team accounts.

HOW TO USE IT

Step 1: Get the Muse app or visit Muse.ai. Sign in using your Meta account (Facebook or Instagram).

Step 2: Connect the apps you want Muse to use. Only connect what you actually need.

Step 3: Give it a task, review the result, and approve before it posts, sends, or spends.

Ready-to-paste prompt:

Here are the photos and details for an item I want to sell. Create a Facebook Marketplace listing with an attention-grabbing title, suggested price based only on the information I provide, and a clear product description. Make it sound professional and trustworthy. Do not publish anything until I review and approve it.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

A finished draft or completed task in minutes, depending on what you ask it to do.

You can have Muse prepare listings, draft emails, set reminders, and work with information from connected apps.

You remain in control. You approve before it posts, sends, or spends.

The free version handles lighter use. Heavy daily use can burn through your allowance.

WHAT YOU'RE MISSING

Here's where the details matter.

Additional limitations:

  • Free usage has a refreshing limit, but Meta does not publish the exact number on its help pages.
  • Meta does not say how many tokens an individual task consumes.
  • Power and Maximum are billed monthly and automatically renew.
  • Muse is still in limited testing and is not available everywhere.
  • You must be 18 or older to subscribe.
  • Meta has not officially documented Marketplace features yet. What works may change.
  • Muse is designed for one person, not shared team accounts.
  • You must check its work before anything goes out.

My advice: Do not jump straight into a $100 subscription. Test the free version first. See whether it actually saves you time.

PRIVACY

This one deserves your attention.

Muse can work with connected apps and information you give it. That means you need to be careful about what you connect and what you ask it to handle.

Do not casually hand over customer information, private business documents, or financial details.

Review Meta's current privacy terms, connected-app permissions, and data-use policies before giving Muse access to sensitive information.

Also, remember that approval is not a substitute for checking its work. Read the listing. Check the price. Review the email. Make sure it did what you asked.

ALTERNATIVES

  • ChatGPT — Worth comparing for writing, research, and supported tasks involving connected tools.
  • Zapier AI — Worth comparing when your goal is connecting business apps and automating repetitive work.

Price

Free: Free: $0 with a Meta account. Usage limit that refreshes (no number published).

Paid: Power $20/month (500M Muse tokens a week); Maximum $100/month (3B a week). Monthly billing, auto-renews. See muse.ai for current pricing.

VERDICT

Worth a try.

Muse is interesting because it moves beyond answering questions and attempts to complete actual tasks.

For small business owners who sell products, manage customers, and juggle daily responsibilities, that could be useful.

But there are real limitations: availability, usage limits, unclear task costs, and features that are not fully documented.

Try free. Test it on something that does not matter if it goes wrong. See what it can actually accomplish.

You do not need another robot giving you a motivational speech about productivity. You need one that takes something off your damn to-do list. Muse might be that robot. Make it prove it.
