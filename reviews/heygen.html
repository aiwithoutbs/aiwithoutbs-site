HeyGen review for small business

HeyGen turns a script, a PDF, a slide deck, or a link into a video with an AI presenter, either a stock one or a digital twin of you. It's free to try with a watermark. Paid plans start at $29 a month and run on credits.

#4Our rank · see the six-rank keyIt's alright

“Videos of you, without filming you every time.” Useful in the right setup. Don't rearrange your life for it. Researched

WHY YOU SHOULD CARE

If you need sales, how-to, or social videos and don't have time to film every one, HeyGen gives you a presenter, a voice, and captions without a camera. You can also record yourself once and let an AI version of you do the talking, and HeyGen says it can translate a finished video into 175+ languages with matched lip movement.

If you don't make videos for your business, skip it.

WHEN TO USE IT

  • You make sales, social, or how-to videos often and filming each one eats your week.
  • You want one video in more than one language.
  • You're comfortable with an AI copy of your face and voice representing your business.
  • You already have scripts, listings, or slide decks sitting around.

WHEN TO SKIP IT

  • You make a handful of videos a year. Use the free plan or your phone.
  • Your customers buy from you because they know you. A real you on camera beats an avatar.
  • You mostly make training videos for staff. Compare Synthesia before you pick.
  • You don't want another monthly subscription that runs on credits.

HOW TO USE IT

Step 1: Sign up for the free plan at heygen.com. Free gives you 3 watermarked videos a month, up to 1 minute each.

Step 2: Start a new video, pick a stock presenter (or upload one clear photo of yourself for a talking photo avatar), and paste your script [CHECK: exact menu names].

Step 3: Use this script for a first test, then generate and watch the result:

Hi, I'm [your name] with [your business]. In under a minute, here's how [your service] works. Step one: [first step]. Step two: [second step]. Step three: [third step]. Questions? Call us at [your number].

WHAT YOU'LL GET

A short video, up to 1 minute on the free plan, with an AI presenter reading your script and HeyGen branding on it. Expect to fix how it says names and local terms, and judge for yourself whether the presenter looks natural enough for your customers.

We have not run our own tests for this review.

WHAT YOU'RE MISSING

The catches:

  • Credits drive everything on paid plans. HeyGen's page doesn't spell out what one credit buys [CHECK].
  • Creator credits roll over one month only. Pro lets you raise credits any time, with rollover.
  • The pricing page lists yearly billing as 2 months free [CHECK: which rate the listed prices show].
  • HeyGen's own pages disagree on counts (500+ presenters on pricing, 1,100+ on features; 175 vs 177+ languages). Treat the numbers as ballpark.

Bottom line: Start free. Pay only once you know you'll make videos every month.

PRIVACY

HeyGen says a Personal Avatar or voice clone only gets made with the person's verified permission: you read a short consent statement on camera. Making one means handing over your face and voice.

We did not read HeyGen's privacy policy or terms for this review [CHECK: how your face, voice, and scripts are stored and used]. Read them before you make an avatar of yourself or an employee.

ALTERNATIVES

  • Synthesia: Similar avatar videos, built more for training. Read our Synthesia review.
  • CapCut: If you'll film yourself and just need captions and quick edits. Read our CapCut review.
  • Loom: If a quick screen recording with your real voice does the job. Read our Loom review.
