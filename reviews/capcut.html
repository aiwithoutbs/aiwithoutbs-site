CapCut review for small business

CapCut is a free video editor for phone, desktop, and web that writes captions for you from what's said in the video. Pro is listed at $19.99 and adds 1,200 AI credits a month. The catch for a business: some templates and music are licensed for personal use only, so check the "commercial" mark before you post an ad.

#4Our rank · see the six-rank keyIt's alright

“Captions your videos so you don't have to type them.” Useful in the right setup. Don't rearrange your life for it. Researched

WHY YOU SHOULD CARE

Short videos with captions are how a lot of small businesses show up on TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts. CapCut listens to your video, types the captions, and lets you trim, resize, and post without paying an editor.

If you don't post video for your business, skip it.

WHEN TO USE IT

  • You post short videos for your business on TikTok, Reels, or YouTube.
  • You want captions without typing them.
  • You shoot on your phone and need fast trims and resizing.
  • You don't want to pay for editing software until you know you'll keep posting.

WHEN TO SKIP IT

  • You don't make video for your business.
  • You need every template and song cleared for paid ads without checking each one.
  • Your company rules don't allow uploading client footage to a service with a broad content license.
  • You record screen walkthroughs, not social clips. A screen recorder fits that job better.

HOW TO USE IT

Step 1: Sign up free at capcut.com and download the desktop app (Windows 10 recommended, or macOS 10.14 and up), or use the web version.

Step 2: Drag your video onto the timeline. Go to Text, pick Auto captions, and click Generate.

Step 3: Read every caption line and fix names and prices. Then click Export and pick your resolution, or share straight to TikTok or YouTube.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

A captioned, trimmed video in the size each social site wants, made in one free app. CapCut says its captions are "almost perfect." That is CapCut's claim, not ours, and its own help text tells you to check names, terms, and timing before you publish.

We have not run our own tests for this review.

WHAT YOU'RE MISSING

Here's how the money works, as listed on CapCut's pricing page.

The catches:

  • CapCut says these prices are "for reference only." Sign in to see what you'd actually pay, plus taxes.
  • The billing period is not shown on the public page [CHECK]. Paid plans can auto-renew until you cancel, per CapCut's terms.
  • AI video generation uses credits, and you can buy more. Watch the meter.

Bottom line: Start free. Only pay for Pro if you're using the AI tools every week and the free captions and edits aren't enough.

PRIVACY

CapCut in the US is run by TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC, per its Terms of Service (updated April 15, 2026). When you submit content, you give it and its partners a worldwide, royalty-free, sub-licensable, perpetual license to use that content for operating, developing, and providing the service, subject to your settings.

Don't upload client footage or anything confidential unless you're fine with those terms. We did not read CapCut's full Privacy Policy for this review [CHECK].

ALTERNATIVES

  • Descript — If you'd rather edit video by editing the transcript, like a Word doc [CHECK: not reviewed here].
  • Canva AI — If you already make your flyers in Canva and want simple video in the same place. Read our Canva AI review.
  • Loom — If your videos are screen walkthroughs for staff or customers, not social posts. Read our Loom review.

VERDICT

It's alright Rank 4 of 6: Useful in the right setup. Don't rearrange your life for it.

Captions and quick social edits for $0, which covers what most small businesses need from it. The license fine print on templates and music is the reason it isn't ranked higher: read the "commercial" mark before anything goes in an ad.
