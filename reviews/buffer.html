Buffer AI Assistant review for small business

Buffer's AI Assistant writes and reworks social media captions right inside Buffer, the post scheduler. It's included on every plan, including Free, with unlimited use. Paid plans start at $5 a month per channel, billed yearly.

#4Our rank · see the six-rank keyIt's alright

“Writes the social post right where you schedule it.” Useful in the right setup. Don't rearrange your life for it. Researched

WHY YOU SHOULD CARE

If you post to Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, or your Google Business Profile and stare at a blank box every time, the AI Assistant drafts the caption where you already schedule it. It can shorten or rephrase a post to fit each network's limits, and it costs nothing extra on any plan.

If you don't post to social media for your business, skip it.

WHEN TO USE IT

  • You already schedule posts in Buffer, or plan to.
  • You post to a few social accounts every week and the blank caption box slows you down.
  • You want one post reworked for each network without copy-pasting between tools.
  • You want AI writing help without paying for another subscription.

WHEN TO SKIP IT

  • You post once in a while. Write it on your phone and post it directly.
  • You already pay for ChatGPT or Claude and are happy pasting captions in.
  • You need images or videos made, not words. Look at Canva first.
  • You manage lots of social accounts and don't want a bill that grows with every one.

HOW TO USE IT

Step 1: Sign up for the free plan at buffer.com and connect up to 3 social accounts.

Step 2: Open a new post, click AI Assistant on the side of the composer, and type a prompt of at least 4 words.

Step 3: Use this prompt for a first test, then click Generate:

Write a friendly Facebook post for [your business] in [your town] announcing [your offer or news]. Audience: [who your customers are]. End with: call [your number] or reply here. Keep it under 80 words.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

A draft caption in a few seconds that you can regenerate, rephrase, shorten, expand, or make more casual or formal before you insert it into your post. Buffer itself says to double-check what it suggests, so expect to fix facts, prices, and anything that doesn't sound like you.

We have not run our own tests for this review.

WHAT YOU'RE MISSING

Here's how the plans line up, per Buffer's pricing page on Nov 28, 2026.

PlanPriceWhat you get
Free$0Up to 3 channels, 10 scheduled posts per channel, AI Assistant
Essentials$5/month per channel, billed yearlyUnlimited scheduled posts, 1 user, AI Assistant
Team$10/month per channel, billed yearlyUnlimited team members, approvals, AI Assistant

The catches:

  • The AI Assistant is the same on every plan. You don't get a better AI by paying more.
  • A channel is one social account. One Facebook page plus one Instagram is 2 channels.
  • Listed paid prices are the yearly rate. Buffer says yearly billing saves you 20% [CHECK: month-to-month price].
  • Channels past 10 cost less each. Nonprofits get 50% off paid plans.

Bottom line: Start free. Pay only if you outgrow 3 accounts or 10 queued posts.

PRIVACY

Whatever you type into the AI Assistant, like your business details and post ideas, goes through Buffer's AI feature. Connecting your social accounts gives Buffer permission to post for you.

We did not read Buffer's privacy policy or terms for this review [CHECK: which AI provider Buffer uses and how your prompts are stored]. Read them before you paste in anything private.

ALTERNATIVES

  • ChatGPT: Writes captions free and works with any scheduler. Read our ChatGPT review.
  • Canva AI: If you need the post graphic, not just the words. Read our Canva AI review.
  • Claude: Strong writer if you want posts that sound less canned. Read our Claude review.

Price

Free: , $0: up to 3 channels, 10 scheduled posts per channel at a time, 100 ideas, 1 user, AI Assistant with unlimited use, per Buffer's pricing page.

Paid: Essentials: $5 a month per channel, billed yearly ($60 a year), unlimited scheduled posts, 1 user. Team: $10 a month per channel, billed yearly ($120 a year), unlimited team members and approval workflows. 14-day free trial on paid plans [CHECK: month-to-month price].

VERDICT

It's alright Rank 4 of 6: Useful in the right setup. Don't rearrange your life for it.

A handy, free caption helper if you already schedule posts in Buffer. It's the same kind of writing help you get from ChatGPT, just closer to the post button, so it's not a reason to sign up on its own. That keeps it at "It's alright."
