Claude review for small business

Claude is the free AI that handles long documents and careful writing without sounding like a robot. Got a contract, a long email chain, or a proposal that has to sound like a grown adult wrote it? This one usually does it cleaner. Only need quick short drafts? Skip it.

Verdict: Worth a try. Open this one when the document is long or the writing has to sound like a real business owner. Use it for the careful stuff, not the quick-and-dirty.

What it's good for

  • Summarize a long contract or PDF so you know the real points.
  • Draft a proposal or estimate that doesn't sound stiff or salesy.
  • Rewrite a messy client email into something clear and professional.
  • Turn rough notes into a clean, organized write-up.
  • Compare two options and lay out the pros and cons without fluff.
  • Clean up a long piece of writing so it reads like a person wrote it.

How to use it

1. Go to claude.ai and start a free chat.
2. Paste this prompt: "Summarize this document in plain English. Pull out the main points, any deadlines, and anything I need to watch out for. Keep it under 200 words: [paste or upload the doc]."
3. Read it, fix anything that doesn't match what you need, then use it.

What you'll get

A clear summary or solid rewrite in a minute or two. It tends to stay closer to the original meaning than some other tools. Check numbers, names, and anything about money or legal language before you rely on it.

What you're missing

  • Free plan limits reset every 5 hours, with a weekly cap on top. Anthropic publishes no message count; it depends on how long and heavy your chats are.
  • The stronger Opus models, Research, and Claude Code are paid only. Pro is $20/month, or $17/month billed yearly ($200). Max is $100/month, Ultra $200/month.
  • Not the fastest for quick one-line answers or real-time social chatter. ChatGPT or Grok can be snappier there.
  • No AI image generator listed on any plan. Need pictures? Use ChatGPT, Grok, or Canva.
  • Still not a lawyer or accountant. Double-check anything that costs you money or binds you.

Price

Free: Free: $0. Limited usage every 5 hours and every week (no fixed number published). Includes web search, file uploads, memory, and up to 5 Projects.

Paid: Pro $20/month, or $17/month billed yearly ($200 up front); Max $100/month; Ultra $200/month. Team (2-150 seats) and Enterprise ($20/seat/month plus usage) also available. Prices exclude tax.
