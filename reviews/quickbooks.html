QuickBooks (Intuit Assist) review for small business

QuickBooks Online now comes with AI built in, which Intuit calls Intuit Intelligence (you may know it as Intuit Assist). It sorts expenses, chases invoices, and answers questions about your numbers. Good if you already run your books in QuickBooks. Not a reason to switch by itself.

#4Our rank · see the six-rank keyIt's alright

“Your bookkeeper gets a sidekick, not a replacement.” Useful in the right setup. Don't rearrange your life for it. Researched

WHY YOU SHOULD CARE

If you already keep your books in QuickBooks, the AI is in there whether you use it or not. It learns how you sort expenses, drafts invoice reminders, and lets you ask questions about your reports in plain English.

You and your accountant still approve what it does. Intuit says it never replaces your accountant.

If you don't use QuickBooks and aren't shopping for bookkeeping software, skip it.

WHEN TO USE IT

  • You already do your books in QuickBooks Online.
  • You spend hours a month sorting transactions or chasing invoices.
  • You want quick answers about your numbers without building reports.
  • Your accountant already works in QuickBooks.

WHEN TO SKIP IT

  • You don't use QuickBooks and aren't switching accounting software.
  • You're on QuickBooks Desktop. This review covers QuickBooks Online.
  • You'd only pay for Plus to get the AI. That's a lot of money for a beta feature set.

HOW TO USE IT

Step 1: Log in to QuickBooks Online and open the AI chat (Intuit lists AI Chat on Simple Start and up, marked beta with limited capacity).

Step 2: Paste this: "Show me my top 5 expense categories for the last 90 days and anything that looks unusual compared to the 90 days before."

Step 3: Click into anything it flags and check the actual transactions before you change a thing.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

Faster sorting of routine transactions and a quick read on your numbers without digging through reports.

Expect to correct it early on while it learns your patterns. Several of the features are labeled beta.

We have not run our own tests for this review.

  • The sale price ends after 3 months.
  • A lot of the AI is marked beta or new.
  • The features that save the most time sit on the higher plans.
  • ACH bill pay costs $0.50 per standard transaction past your monthly allotment.

Bottom line: Use the AI in the plan you already have. Upgrade for your books, not for the AI.

PRIVACY

Intuit says you and your accountant stay in control: you review and edit AI-drafted messages, estimates, reminders, and bills, and you decide on its suggested fixes.

We did not review Intuit's data and AI training policy for this review [CHECK]. Read it before you connect client or payroll data.

ALTERNATIVES

  • Xero — Another cloud bookkeeping option if you're choosing from scratch [CHECK pricing].
  • ChatGPT or Claude — Can explain an exported report, but don't touch your books.
  • Your accountant — Still the one who should sign off on taxes.

Price

Free: QuickBooks Free is $0/month with no credit card, but has no mobile app or third-party integrations, per Intuit's pricing page. Which AI features it includes isn't listed [CHECK]. Paid plans offer a 30-day trial window for phone support.

Paid: List prices per month: Simple Start $38 (1 user), Essentials $85 (3 users), Plus $140 (5 users), Advanced $340 (25 users). Intuit was showing 70% off for the first 3 months ($11.40 / $25.50 / $42 / $102) on Nov 18, 2026. Confirm at checkout.
