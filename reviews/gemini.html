Google Gemini review for small business

Google's free AI that works best if your email and files already live in Google. Here's the deal: use it when you're already in their world and just need a quick draft or search without jumping tools.

Researched Verdict: Worth a try.

WHY YOU SHOULD CARE

If your business already runs on Gmail and Drive, this one can help you write and research without leaving the place you already work. Free to start. The stronger models and Gmail integration cost money. It still talks like it knows more than it does. You still make the final call.

WHEN TO USE IT

  • Draft an email without starting from scratch (Gemini inside Gmail itself needs a paid plan).
  • Summarize a long document or file you upload.
  • Do a quick web search and get a short answer with sources.
  • Make a simple image for a post or flyer.
  • Research a topic and get a clean summary with Deep Research.
  • Answer everyday questions while you're already in Google.

WHEN TO SKIP IT

  • Long, careful document work. Claude is often better there.
  • You don't already live in Google and just need a basic writing bot.
  • Heavy daily use if you refuse to pay.

HOW TO USE IT

1 Go to gemini.google.com (use the same Google account you use for email).

2 Paste this prompt: "Summarize this long document I just uploaded. Pull out the main points, any deadlines, and anything I need to watch out for. Keep it under 200 words."

3 Read it, change anything that doesn't sound like you, then use it.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

A usable draft or short summary in under a minute. Check names, prices, and facts. Free runs a lighter model (Flash-Lite); stronger models, more use, and Gemini inside Gmail and Docs cost money.

WHAT YOU'RE MISSING

Free plan runs the lighter Flash-Lite model with usage limits. Google publishes no exact number; paid plans just say 2x or 4x Free. Paid (US): Google AI Plus $4.99/month, AI Pro $19.99/month ($199.99/year), AI Ultra $99.99 or $199.99/month. Plus isn't sold in every country. Gemini inside Gmail and Docs is paid only, and Google AI plans need a personal Google account. Workspace business accounts need Gemini Enterprise (from $21/seat/month). Not the strongest pick for long, careful documents. Still not a lawyer or accountant. Double-check anything that costs you money or binds you.

PRIVACY

Chats can be used to improve the models unless you adjust settings; don't dump sensitive client or financial details you wouldn't want floating around.

ALTERNATIVES

Claude: better when the document is long and the wording has to sound like a grown adult wrote it. ChatGPT: more consistent for quick short drafts if you're not already living in Google.

Price

Free: Free: $0 with a Google account. Flash-Lite model, image generation, Deep Research, 15 GB storage. Usage is limited (no exact number published).

Paid: US: Google AI Plus $4.99/month (400 GB, Gemini in Gmail); AI Pro $19.99/month or $199.99/year (Pro model, Gmail/Docs, 5 TB); AI Ultra $99.99 or $199.99/month. Business: Gemini Enterprise from $21/seat/month. No partial refunds.

Sources (official, checked Nov 4, 2026):

  • https://gemini.google/subscriptions/
  • https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/

Official site: https://gemini.google.com

VERDICT

Worth a try.

Open this one if your business already runs on Google. Just don't expect the free version to keep up once you start leaning on it for anything beyond quick drafts and searches.
