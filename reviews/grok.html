Grok review for small business

Grok is an AI chat from xAI that answers questions, writes, and looks things up on the web and X. Draft emails, summarize notes, or check what's happening now without opening five tabs. If you don't need that, skip it.

Verdict: Worth a try. It's free to start and useful for everyday drafts and quick lookups if you treat the output as a first pass, not the final word.

What it's good for

  • Draft a polite reply to a customer complaint or late-payment email.
  • Turn rough notes from a job into a clean summary and next steps.
  • Write a short Facebook or Nextdoor post about a service you offer.
  • Look up recent prices, local rules, or what people are saying about a topic on X.
  • Summarize a long PDF or contract so you know the main points.
  • Make a simple image for a post or flyer (the free plan has a limited number).

How to use it

1. Go to grok.com or the X app and start a free chat.
2. Paste this prompt: "Write a short, calm reply to this customer email. Keep it under 100 words and end with a clear next step: [paste the email]."
3. Read it, change anything that doesn't sound like you, then send.

What you'll get

A usable first draft or quick summary in under a minute. You still need to check names, prices, and facts before you use it. On the free plan you can do the basics; heavier use and video creation sit behind paid plans.

What you're missing

  • The free plan has usage limits. xAI doesn't publish an exact number; it just says "Limited." When you hit the limit, you wait or upgrade.
  • It can sound confident when it's wrong, especially on fast-moving news or numbers.
  • Video generation needs a paid plan. Paid tiers: Plus $8/month, Premium $30/month, Super $100/month, Ultra $200/month.
  • It is not the strongest choice for long, careful document work. Claude is often better for that.
  • Always double-check anything that involves money, legal, or medical claims.

Price

Free: Free: $0/month, with limited daily use of Grok chat and image generation (no exact limit published).

Paid: Plus $8/month ($80/year); Premium $30/month ($300/year, 4x Plus usage, image and video); Super $100/month; Ultra $200/month. Prices exclude tax. See grok.com for current pricing.
